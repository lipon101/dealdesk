DealDrive review: the fast answer

⚡ Information gain — 30-second summary

DealDrive is an India-built agentic AI CRM aimed squarely at SMEs: $69 lifetime buys AI lead scoring, automated follow-up drafting, contact enrichment, contracts and e-signatures in one pipeline, positioning it as a cheaper, AI-first alternative to HubSpot and Pipedrive for teams under twenty reps.

That is the short version. The longer version matters, because “AI CRM” can mean anything from a renamed dashboard to genuinely useful automation.

Our analysis shows DealDrive earns its keep in one job above all: catching deals that go quiet and drafting the follow-up before they go cold.

What DealDrive actually does

DealDrive is an AI-powered CRM built for SMEs by founder Himanshu Singh, who spent a decade in B2B sales before launching the company in Gurgaon, India in July 2021. It is positioned against HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Zoho CRM.

Instead of a passive database, it layers AI on top of the whole sales loop:

  • AI lead scoring — score leads and forecast revenue from pipeline signals in real time
  • Automated follow-ups — the AI catches stalled deals, drafts personalized outreach, and nudges deals forward
  • Contact enrichment & contracts — enrich contacts, manage contracts, and send e-signatures without leaving the CRM
  • Pipeline visibility — revenue won, active deals, open leads, and pipeline value on one real-time dashboard

Setup is a standard sign-up. The AppSumo listing notes some users hit a 14-day Pro trial prompt on activation — a flow worth knowing about before you buy, since the lifetime license is what you are actually paying for.

👎 Watch out

Frequently asked questions

What does the AI in DealDrive actually do?

It scores leads, catches stalled deals, drafts personalized follow-up outreach, and coaches your team in real time — per the AppSumo product page and our testing of the pipeline.

Can I manage contracts and e-signatures inside DealDrive?

Yes. Contract management and e-signatures are built in, alongside contact enrichment, so deals do not need a separate documents tool.

How many license tiers does the AppSumo deal have?

Three tiers from $69 to $249. Tier 1 maps to the Starter plan; Tiers 2–3 map to the Pro plan family with more capacity.

Is DealDrive a replacement for HubSpot or Pipedrive?

For small teams it can replace the pipeline core of either. If you depend on HubSpot’s marketing suite or a deep app marketplace, weigh DealDrive’s leaner integration surface first.

Is there a refund window?
