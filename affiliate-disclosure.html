Affiliate disclosure

The short version: some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. This is how the site is funded.

What that means for you

  • Prices are set by the vendor — affiliate links never change what you pay.
  • We only link to products we have actually evaluated. Every “Check the price” button sits inside a review that names what we tested and what we couldn’t verify.
  • We do not accept payment for coverage, placement, or positive reviews. Sponsored content, if it ever exists, will be clearly labeled.

Which links are affiliate links?

Buttons and links that go out to a software marketplace are affiliate links and are marked with an AFF tag or an affiliate label. Each article also carries its own disclosure notice near the top. When you hover a marked link you’ll see rel="sponsored nofollow" — the standard machine-readable disclosure search engines expect.

Does an affiliate relationship change your verdicts?

No. Our review protocol is fixed before any commission is considered: we test the product, we verify deal terms against the official product page on the day of publication, and we name who should skip the deal. A guide that ends with “don’t buy this” earns nothing — and that is exactly what some of our guides conclude.
