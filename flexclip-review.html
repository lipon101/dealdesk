What FlexClip actually does

FlexClip is an easy-to-use video editor that runs in the browser on Windows, Mac and Linux, with a mobile companion for image tools. It offers over 4,000 templates for education, business, marketing, festivals, special events and social media, plus access to more than 4 million royalty-free videos, music and photos.

The toolkit splits into six areas:

  • Templates — 4,000+ searchable starting points for every social platform and use case
  • Editing basics — trim, split, combine, speed control, reverse, transitions, animated text and overlays
  • AI tools — AI subtitles, text-to-video, AI voiceover, AI script and image generation (credits-based)
  • Screen & webcam recorder — capture tutorials, meetings and presentations
  • Brand kit — consistent logos, colors and fonts across exports, plus custom font uploads
  • Stock library — millions of royalty-free videos, music and photos with commercial-use rights

Unlike desktop editors, there is no install — you open the browser, pick a template and start cutting.

Feature deep dive

The AI tools and credit economy

FlexClip’s AI layer includes subtitle generation, text-to-video, AI voiceover, script writing and image generation, all running on a credits system. In our testing the AI subtitles were the most useful day-to-day feature — with the caveat that projects with background music or multiple tracks need cleanup (FlexClip’s own guidance recommends a single vocal track and clips under 20 minutes).

Screen and webcam recording

The built-in recorder captures screen, webcam or both — handy for tutorials and client walkthroughs — and drops you straight into the editor timeline, which is a smoother flow than record-in-one-app-edit-in-another.

Brand kit and commercial rights

For businesses, the brand kit (logo, colors, fonts) plus custom font upload keeps every export on-message. Commercial-use rights and reseller rights on the core features mean agencies and freelancers can produce client work legitimately, and there is no FlexClip watermark on any paid tier.

Frequently asked questions

What is FlexClip?

FlexClip is an easy-to-use video editor for Windows, Mac and Linux that runs in the browser. It offers 4,000+ templates, access to 4M+ royalty-free videos/music/photos, animated text, widgets, overlays, intros/outros, screen recording and AI tools.

What browsers does FlexClip support?

Google Chrome, Firefox and Safari. FlexClip recommends Chrome for optimal stability and functionality.

Can I use FlexClip on mobile?

You can use image tools on mobile, but for generating and editing videos FlexClip recommends a PC or Mac browser.

How do I record my screen with FlexClip?

Go to Media > Record > Record Screen or Webcam, select “Screen Only,” adjust settings, start recording, choose the screen to share, then stop and review before editing.

Why are my AI subtitles incomplete?

AI subtitle generation can fail on projects with multiple audio tracks, original background music, or very long clips. Keep a single vocal track, separate vocals from music, and keep clips under 20 minutes for best results.

What happens if I do not redeem my AppSumo code in time?

Codes must be redeemed within 60 days of purchase. If you miss the window, contact FlexClip support for manual stacking help (required for pre-2025 purchases anyway).

Can I get a refund if it is not for me?

Yes. AppSumo’s “We Got Your Back” guarantee covers this deal for 60 days.
