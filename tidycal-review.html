What TidyCal actually does

TidyCal is a scheduling and booking platform created by AppSumo as a simpler, more affordable alternative to Calendly. The team says it built TidyCal after Calendly and similar tools felt too expensive and overloaded for solo businesses.

Instead of a bare calendar link, it layers four capabilities into one product:

  • Unlimited booking types — one-on-one, group, and recurring bookings with no caps on how many you create
  • Payments built in — sell packages and subscriptions, take Stripe or PayPal, and get paid at the time of booking
  • Custom availability & branding — time blocks, buffers, booking limits, coupon codes, and your own booking page
  • Client reviews & reminders — collect post-session reviews and automate email notifications

Setup is a simple sign-up with a password — the AppSumo license attaches to the email address on your order. The first-run experience is genuinely quick: create a booking type, share the link, done.

Feature deep dive: what stands out

The AppSumo listing promotes four headline capabilities. Here is what each one does in practice.

Unlimited booking types without the upsell

Calendly-style tools typically meter how many event types you can run. TidyCal does not — one-on-one, group, and recurring bookings are all unlimited, and recurring setups cover weekly check-ins, workshops, and retainers.

Each booking type gets custom availability, buffer time, and booking limits, so the calendar works the way your business does rather than the other way around.

Payments at the point of booking

This is TidyCal’s signature differentiator. Clients can pay for sessions when they book — packages and subscriptions included — through Stripe or PayPal, with coupon codes for promotions.

In our testing, the flow was straightforward: create a paid booking type, connect Stripe, and the client hits a checkout before the slot is confirmed. For coaches and consultants that removes a whole invoice-chasing step.

Group sessions and recurring bookings

Group bookings let several attendees claim slots on one link — workshops, webinars, and team onboarding included. Recurring booking types handle weekly sessions without re-creating events.

The product page highlights these as first-class features, and in our testing they behaved as advertised: set capacity, share the link, and let the calendar fill.

Client reviews and brand controls

TidyCal can collect client reviews after sessions and offers a booking page you can brand. Coupon codes, custom availability, and notification controls round out the set.

Integrations cover Google, PayPal, Stripe, Zapier, and Zoom — with Google Meet flows for friction-free video calls. Zoom and Google Meet links attach to bookings automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Is TidyCal really a lifetime deal with no subscription?

Yes. You pay once for lifetime access with all future Lifetime Plan updates. There is no recurring fee, and because AppSumo built the product, the “will they shut down?” risk that hangs over most lifetime deals is much lower.

Can I collect payments through TidyCal?

Yes. You can sell packages and subscriptions and accept payments via Stripe or PayPal, getting paid at the time of booking. Coupon codes are supported for promotions.

Does TidyCal limit how many booking types I can create?

No. One-on-one, group, and recurring booking types are unlimited, with custom availability, buffers, and booking limits per type.

Can I upgrade my license later?

Yes, while the deal is available you can upgrade between the two license tiers. Downgrades are only possible within 60 days of purchase.

What happens if I do not activate within 60 days?

You must activate your license within 60 days of purchase. If you miss that window you lose the license, so redeem it right away even if you plan to set the tool up later.

Is there a refund window?

Yes. TidyCal is refundable up to 60 days under AppSumo’s “We Got Your Back” guarantee, so you can test it against your real calendar and still walk away if it disappoints.
