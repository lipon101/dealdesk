What Viloud actually does

Viloud lets you create and stream your own live, linear, and on-demand TV channels without coding. Upload videos for cloud hosting or add video links from YouTube, Vimeo, or your own hosting, then manage channels and content from one dashboard. Linear channels provide continuous programming — content plays on a schedule like a real TV channel — and on-demand (VOD) channels let viewers pick what to watch.

The platform splits into five areas:

  • Linear TV channels — schedule videos to play in sequence with no viewer interaction needed; ideal for lobbies, retail, digital signage and brand channels
  • On-demand (VOD) channels — let viewers choose from your library, presented in your branded player
  • Cloud video hosting — storage minutes scale with tier, plus M3U8 output links on higher tiers for external players
  • Channel websites — up to 50 websites with custom domains (CNAME) on the top tier, so each channel can live on its own branded URL
  • Monetization & analytics — ad scheduling and view analytics built into the dashboard

There are also team members and workspaces, YouTube/Vimeo link importing, and the ability to embed the player anywhere. The pitch: wish your audience could follow your video content like they follow their favorite TV show? Viloud is that follow-able channel.

TV-channel tools are judged by what viewers actually experience, so we ran a controlled protocol:

The results: creating a scheduled linear channel is straightforward, playback was smooth in the embedded player, and the M3U8 output on higher tiers opens real broadcast workflows. The main planning burden is content volume — a linear channel consumes your library fast, which makes the storage-minute tiers and YouTube/Vimeo importing important.

Feature deep dive

Linear channels that run themselves

A linear channel plays a continuous schedule — no clicks, no “up next” decisions. That makes it ideal for digital signage, retail displays, event loops, waiting-room screens and brand channels that should always be “on air.” You build the playlist once and Viloud keeps the programming flowing.

On-demand channels and branded players

VOD channels present your library for viewer choice in a player you can embed on your own site. Combined with custom-domain channel websites (3 on Tier 1, up to 50 on Tier 10), each audience segment can get its own branded destination.

YouTube and Vimeo as sources

You do not have to rehost everything: add video links from YouTube, Vimeo, or your own hosting and build channels from existing libraries. This is the feature that makes Viloud practical for marketers with a back catalog already living elsewhere.

Monetization and M3U8

Ad scheduling is part of the platform, and higher tiers add M3U8 output links so you can feed external players or broadcast workflows. Team members and workspaces (up to 5 workspaces and 10 team members on Tier 10) keep channel operations manageable.
