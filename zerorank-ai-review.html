ZeroRank AI review: the fast answer

⚡ Information gain — 30-second summary

ZeroRank AI is a young but sharply focused AI-search tracker: $69 lifetime buys citation and sentiment monitoring for your brand across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Grok, with prioritized on-page, off-page and technical fixes that most general SEO suites do not offer yet.

That is the short version. The longer version matters, because AI-search visibility is a new category and the tools in it vary wildly in maturity.

Our analysis shows ZeroRank earns its keep in one job above all: telling you whether AI assistants mention your brand at all — and what to fix when they do not.

What ZeroRank AI actually does

ZeroRank AI is an AI-search visibility platform founded in Tirana, Albania in July 2025 by Kelvin Cobanaj, who built it after two decades of crafting online products. It is bootstrapped, and the founder publishes regular updates on the AppSumo listing.

Where classic SEO tracks Google rankings, ZeroRank tracks something newer: whether AI answer engines cite your brand, and how they describe it.

  • Citation monitoring — brand mentions, citations, and sentiment across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Grok
  • Competitor benchmarking — compare who AI platforms recommend in your niche and why
  • Prompt tracking — monitor how your brand performs across tracked prompts over time
  • Prioritized recommendations — AI-visibility gaps turned into on-page, off-page, and technical actions

Setup is straightforward: create an account, add your brand and keywords, and let the platform run its first scans. The dashboard then shows where you appear in AI answers — and where competitors beat you.

Feature deep dive: what stands out

The AppSumo listing promotes four headline capabilities. Here is what each one does in practice.

Cross-platform AI citation tracking

ZeroRank monitors brand mentions and citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Grok. That breadth matters: your brand can rank on Google and still be invisible in AI answers.

In our testing, the dashboard clearly showed where our test brand appeared, where it was missing, and how sentiment was trending.

From visibility gap to prioritized fix

The standout feature is the recommendation engine: AI-visibility gaps are converted into prioritized on-page, off-page, and technical actions. Instead of “get cited more,” you get concrete steps.

The founder’s updates also note that every AppSumo tier got doubled prompt-tracking limits shortly after launch — a sign of an engaged team that ships.

Workspaces for agencies and multi-brand teams

Workspaces were a top community request and shipped early: each brand gets its own prompts, tracking, and recommendations under one account. Tier 1 includes one workspace, with more seats and workspaces on higher tiers.

For agencies running multiple brands, this separation is the difference between usable and chaotic.

Future AI models note

The deal terms state access to all future AI models may be provided at a discounted rate, or may require an add-on. If you are buying mainly for today’s supported platforms, that is fine; if you expect every future model for free, temper that expectation.

What AI platforms does ZeroRank AI track?

Brand mentions, citations, and sentiment are monitored across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Grok, per the AppSumo product page.
