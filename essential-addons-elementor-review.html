Frequently asked questions

Does Essential Addons work with the free version of Elementor?

Yes. The plugin is built to extend the free Elementor page builder — that is its core purpose. You do not need Elementor Pro to use the widgets.

Tier 1 covers 5 sites for $49; Tier 2 covers 25 sites for $99. Both are lifetime licenses with all future updates.
