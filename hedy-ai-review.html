What Hedy AI actually does

Hedy AI is a real-time AI assistant for meetings and conversations. Instead of waiting for the meeting to end to produce a transcript, Hedy listens as you talk and provides automatic suggestions in the moment — answers, objections, next questions — so you can act while the conversation is still happening.

The platform splits into five areas:

  • Real-time suggestions — automatic guidance during calls based on what is actually being said
  • Specialized modes — purpose-tuned behavior for sales calls, interviews, therapy, negotiations and more
  • Automatic prep notes — briefings generated before a meeting so you walk in ready
  • Post-session summaries — clean recaps, organized notes and data export after the conversation
  • Organize, connect & chat — a web app that stores sessions, connects context and lets you chat with what was discussed

Under the hood, Hedy supports 30+ languages, runs on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows (Beta) and web, includes an Apple Watch companion app, and offers on-device speech recognition with custom vocabulary support for privacy. You can also import and analyze YouTube videos — a useful research feature beyond live meetings.

How we tested the platform

A meeting assistant must be judged live, so we ran a controlled protocol:

Prepare: generate automatic prep notes for a mock sales conversation

Run live: use the sales mode in a simulated call and rate the timing and relevance of suggestions

Review: check the post-session summary, data export, and session chat in the web app

The results: prep notes were genuinely useful, live suggestions arrived quickly and felt contextual in the sales mode, and the summary cleaned up the conversation well. It takes a few sessions to trust the interface — one reviewer put it as “it does what it says on the tin, it takes a while to get used to its interface” — but the real-time layer is the differentiator.

Feature deep dive

Real-time suggestions, not just transcripts

Most meeting tools record and summarize afterward. Hedy works during the conversation: it listens and surfaces suggestions, follow-ups and answers while there is still time to use them. For sales reps and interviewers, that timing is the whole point.

Specialized modes

Hedy’s modes tune behavior to context — sales, interviews, therapy, negotiations and more — so suggestions match the stakes of the conversation instead of generic advice. This is what separates it from a single-purpose recorder.

On-device speech recognition

For privacy-sensitive work, Hedy supports on-device speech recognition with custom vocabulary. Combined with data export and session controls, that gives professionals a way to use AI assistance without shipping raw audio to a cloud vendor.

Platforms and companion features

Hedy spans iOS, Android, macOS, Windows (Beta) and the web, with an Apple Watch companion and YouTube import & analysis. It supports 30+ languages, unlimited sessions and unlimited meeting length on the Pro plan.
