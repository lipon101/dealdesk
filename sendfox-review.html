SendFox review: the fast answer

⚡ Information gain — 30-second summary

SendFox is a creator-first email tool with a genuinely simple lifetime model — 300,000 sends and 5,000 contacts for a $29 one-time AppSumo fee — and it is the cheapest way we have tested for a blogger or YouTuber to stop paying a monthly per-subscriber email bill.

That is the short version. The longer version is about what you trade for that price.

Our analysis shows SendFox covers the creator essentials — campaigns, automations, forms, landing pages and a CRM contact view — while staying deliberately simpler than the marketing-automation suites.

What SendFox actually does

SendFox was created by AppSumo (it is the company’s own product) specifically for content creators who found traditional email platforms confusing and expensive. Instead of charging per subscriber every month, SendFox charges for what you actually use: sends.

The feature set splits into six areas:

  • Scheduled & smart campaigns — one-off broadcasts to your list, with the option to send on a schedule
  • Triggered automations — unlimited automated sequences for onboarding, tags and welcome flows
  • Forms & landing pages — unlimited signup forms and landing pages to grow your list
  • CRM contact view — see what each contact has done without a separate sales CRM
  • Referral widget — turn subscribers into promoters with a built-in referral tool
  • API & AI-agent access — connect your stack, with translation into 30+ languages

SendFox is aimed at the creator who sends a newsletter or launch emails — not at an e-commerce brand running complex journey maps.

Email tools are judged by the send, so we ran a real campaign rather than clicking through the UI.

The results were exactly what the target user needs: a straightforward send experience, automations that fire on schedule, and a contact view that shows who opened and who dropped off — all without the feature sprawl of a heavyweight marketing suite.

Feature deep dive

The lifetime-sends model

SendFox’s pricing is the product. Rather than paying per subscriber forever, you buy sends — 300,000 lifetime sends with this deal — and top up only when you run out. For a creator sending a weekly newsletter to 1,000 subscribers, 300,000 sends is years of email for one $29 payment.

Automations and the CRM contact view

Unlimited triggered automations cover the standard flows — welcome, onboarding, tag-based sequences — and the CRM contact view shows each subscriber’s history in one place. It is not a sales CRM, and it is not meant to be: it is enough context to run a list well.

Forms, landing pages and translation

Unlimited forms and landing pages make list growth self-contained, and translation into 30+ languages is a quiet differentiator for international creators. The API and AI-agent access mean you can wire SendFox into the rest of your stack.
