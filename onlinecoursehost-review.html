What OnlineCourseHost.com actually does

OnlineCourseHost.com is a platform that lets you sell online courses and unlimited memberships, with unlimited students, storage and admin accounts. You get a course website builder, community features, and e-commerce that charges payments directly through Stripe and PayPal — no revenue share, no transaction cut from the platform itself.

The platform splits into seven areas:

  • Course creation — videos, quizzes, assignments, digital downloads, text lessons and audio, with SCORM support
  • AI Course Creator Assistant — helps build courses and marketing content faster
  • AI Student Assistant — supports learners inside the course experience
  • Branded course websites — a website builder per course site with custom domains (ALIAS/DNS guided setup)
  • E-commerce — Stripe and PayPal checkout, coupons, and access to promotional tools
  • Community & engagement — community + website builder, bulk emails, affiliate program, shareable student badges
  • Credentials — verifiable certificates and a helpdesk for support

External video hosting is required (YouTube, Vimeo, bunny.net or any other), which keeps the platform lean on bandwidth. Integrations include Pabbly, SendFox, Stripe, PayPal and Zapier — connecting to over 1,000 apps.
