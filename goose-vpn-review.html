What GOOSE VPN actually does

GOOSE VPN is a consumer VPN service from the Netherlands that routes your connection through encrypted tunnels to servers in 24 countries, hiding your IP address and encrypting traffic on public networks. It positions itself as easy enough for non-technical users while still offering protocol choices for the curious.

The platform splits into five areas:

  • Server network — over 100 servers worldwide in 24 countries, with all future servers included
  • Apps & devices — iOS, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux, Router and Android TV apps, with all future apps included
  • Protocols — IKEv2, L2TP/IPSec, PPTP and OpenVPN support
  • Privacy extras — cyber alarm add-on (Tier 3), no-log claims per the service
  • Simultaneous devices — 3 on Tier 1, 10 on Tier 2, unlimited on Tier 3

There is also unlimited bandwidth on every tier, country-level switching with all supported countries included, and the ability to upgrade or downgrade between the three license tiers after purchase. The pitch is straightforward: a European VPN, owned and operated transparently, at a price that undercuts the big names.

Feature deep dive

Simplicity as a feature

Reviewers describe the apps as easy to use — connect and go. For users who find mainstream VPN apps cluttered, the minimal interface is a genuine advantage, and the router and Android TV support covers whole-home and living-room use.

Protocol choices

GOOSE VPN supports IKEv2, L2TP/IPSec, PPTP and OpenVPN. IKEv2 offers the best speed on newer devices; OpenVPN is the compatibility workhorse. The FAQ explains each protocol’s trade-offs, which is helpful for users who care about the technical layer.

Unlimited bandwidth, device tiers

Every tier includes unlimited bandwidth. The tiers differ only in simultaneous devices — 3, 10, or unlimited — plus the cyber alarm add-on on Tier 3. For a family or multi-device household, Tier 3’s unlimited-device allowance is the clear upgrade.
