What LeadRocks actually does

LeadRocks is a B2B database and contact-finding tool that provides access to 8 billion company and contact records. You search by industry, location, company size, job title and more, then export what you find for outreach. It is designed to replace the “scrape LinkedIn then clean it yourself” workflow with one searchable source.

The toolkit splits into four areas:

  • Contact & company search — filter by industry, location, company size, job title, and more, then view verified details
  • LinkedIn enrichment — find contact info for people and companies from LinkedIn profiles
  • Email verification — validate that the addresses you export are deliverable
  • Extension & CRM integrations — browser extension plus integrations for outreach workflows

Unlike a pure CRM, LeadRocks is a data source: you search, enrich and export to your sequencer or spreadsheet.

How we tested the service

Lead databases are only worth reviewing with a real outbound scenario, so we used one: “find marketing decision-makers at SaaS companies in Europe.”

Search: apply industry, location and title filters and review result relevance

Enrich: pull contact details for a sample of companies and check fields returned

Verify & export: verify a sample of found emails and export the clean set to CSV

The results: filters returned relevant companies, enrichment filled in the expected fields on most records, and the built-in verification flagged a meaningful share of the found emails as undeliverable — which is exactly why you should not skip that step. Coverage for niche roles and smaller companies was thinner than for mainstream titles, as with every database in this category.

Feature deep dive

Search depth and the credit economy

LeadRocks claims 8 billion company and contact records, and search filters cover industry, location, company size and job titles. The credit system is the real pricing lever: each credit roughly equals one contact export, so the ten AppSumo tiers are really about how many contacts you can pull, not which features unlock.

LinkedIn enrichment

The enrichment workflow takes a company or LinkedIn profile and returns contact details for the people behind it. It is the closest thing in the tool to “done-for-you” prospecting, and it pairs naturally with the verification feature before you export.

Built-in verification

Verification runs on the emails you find so your outbound campaigns do not waste sends on dead addresses. In our test it caught a real share of undeliverables — and because unverified exports can hurt your deliverability later, this feature earns its place in the workflow.

Who should buy it (and who should skip)

  • 8 billion records with search, enrichment and verification in one place
  • Verification built in — protect your sender reputation before export
  • 95% discount headline — the lifetime price undercuts most per-seat databases
  • Ten stackable tiers — credits pool for growing teams and agencies
  • LinkedIn enrichment saves the manual profile-by-profile workflow
  • 371 AppSumo reviews at a 4.47 average

👎 Watch out

  • Credit economy adds up — exports, enrichment and verification all consume credits
  • Coverage is thinner for niche roles and smaller companies
  • No true native CRM — you export to your sequencer or spreadsheet
  • Database freshness varies — always verify before a big send

Buy it if you prospect regularly and want a lifetime-priced source with verification built in. Skip it if you need a full sales CRM — LeadRocks is the data layer, not the workflow layer.
