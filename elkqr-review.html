What ElkQR actually does

ElkQR is a QR code management platform offering dynamic and static QR codes with auto-generated SSL and CDN. Dynamic QR codes let you change the destination after the code is printed — the code stays the same, the link behind it does not have to — which is the feature that makes print and packaging campaigns survivable.

The platform splits into five areas:

  • Dynamic & static QR codes — unlimited of both, with custom link aliases and multiple download formats (PNG, JPEG, WEBP, SVG) in sizes up to 4,096 px
  • Bulk creation — generate many codes at once with row limits from 80 to 220 per import by tier
  • Scan controls — one-time scannable codes, scheduled codes, pause/resume, and scan limits via advanced settings
  • Tracking & analytics — unlimited stat retention, pixel tracking, and public stats sharing for transparency
  • Landing pages & deep links — custom landing pages per code plus 100+ apps supported for deep linking

There are also workspaces (10 to unlimited by tier), custom domains with auto SSL, and bulk row limits that scale with your plan. The pitch is simple: a reliable dynamic QR solution for teams that print, ship or campaign at scale.

Feature deep dive

Dynamic codes that survive print

The core differentiator is the editable destination: point a code at a URL, print 10,000 posters, then change where the code leads whenever the campaign changes. Combined with auto-generated SSL and CDN, your codes load fast and stay valid.

One-time and scheduled scans

ElkQR supports one-time scannable QR codes and scheduled codes, with pause/resume controls and scan limits configured in advanced settings. For ticketing, product authentication, or limited promos, this turns a QR code into a controlled redemption token rather than a permanent open door.

Bulk creation and workspaces

Bulk QR creation handles 80 rows per import on Tier 1, 120 on Tier 2 and 220 on Tier 3. Workspaces scale from 10 to unlimited, and custom domains from 10 to unlimited — which is what agencies need to keep client codes separated and branded.

Tracking that closes the loop

With unlimited stat retention and pixel tracking, ElkQR connects physical scans to ad-platform audiences. Public stats sharing is a nice touch for reporting to clients or stakeholders without handing over dashboard logins.

Frequently asked questions

What is ElkQR?

ElkQR is a QR code management platform offering dynamic and static QR codes, bulk creation, scan limits, pixel tracking and custom landing pages — with unlimited codes and scans on every paid plan.

What is the difference between dynamic and static QR codes?

Dynamic QR codes let you change the destination URL after the code is created (even after printing); static codes encode the destination permanently at creation.

How do I set scan limits on a QR code?

Create or edit a QR code, open Advanced Settings, enable the “One-Time/Limited Scans” option, and specify the maximum number of scans allowed.

Are QR codes and scans really unlimited?

Yes — all three AppSumo tiers include unlimited QR codes, unlimited scans and unlimited stat retention.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Tiers include 10, 40, or unlimited custom domains with auto-generated SSL, so your codes can carry your brand.
