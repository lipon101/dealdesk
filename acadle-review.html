What Acadle actually does

Acadle is a learning management platform that lets you create, host and sell online courses under your own brand — with your own domain, logo and colors, no “Powered by Acadle” required. It is built for course creators, coaches and agencies that want to own the learning experience rather than rent a page on a marketplace platform.

The toolkit splits into five areas:

  • White-label academy — host everything on your own domain with full brand control
  • Course builder — video and content lessons with quizzes and certificates
  • Communities — built-in discussion spaces so learning happens alongside others
  • Micro-sites — create unlimited websites for courses, funnels and landing pages
  • SCORM support — upload existing SCORM content on higher tiers

Acadle’s positioning is “everything Kajabi and Thinkific do, without the monthly subscription.”

Feature deep dive

True white-label on your own domain

This is the headline feature: your academy runs on your domain with your branding and no platform attribution. For agencies building courses for clients, that is the difference between a deliverable and a referral to a competitor.

Communities and micro-sites

Acadle bundles community spaces and unlimited micro-sites, so a course launch can include its own landing page and discussion area without stitching three tools together. It is a lightweight ecosystem rather than a full funnel suite — enough for most course launches, not a Kajabi replacement for complex funnels.

SCORM support

SCORM upload support means existing training content from other LMSs can move into Acadle — a meaningful feature for corporate trainers and agencies migrating client content, available on the mid and upper tiers.
