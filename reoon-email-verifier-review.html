What Reoon Email Verifier actually does

Reoon Email Verifier is an email validation service led by Farhan, its lead developer, who introduced it to the AppSumo community in September 2022. Instead of sending test emails, Reoon verifies each address against the receiving mail server (MTA validation) and runs algorithmic checks on the address itself.

The platform’s core capabilities:

  • MTA validation of domains — checks whether the domain and its mail server exist and accept mail
  • Individual inbox status detection — goes beyond deliverability to detect states like storage-full (soft bounce) on Gmail and others
  • Role account detection — flags addresses like info@ or support@ that no single person owns
  • Disposable / temp email detection — proprietary algorithms catch throwaway addresses in real time
  • Spamtrap and complaint detection — flags addresses that could poison your sender reputation
  • API with QUICK and POWER modes — QUICK validates in ~0.5 seconds for live registration checks

Verification happens in bulk without sending emails, keeping your list healthy and your sender reputation intact.

Feature deep dive

Accuracy and the soft-bounce edge

Reoon claims an average accuracy of over 99% for verified emails of mixed quality. In our testing the differentiator was the detail: rather than a binary valid/invalid, you get the receiving server’s state — including soft bounces like a full inbox on Gmail, which most verifiers miss or mislabel.

Disposable email detection

Temp-mail domains churn constantly, which makes static blocklists useless. Reoon’s dynamic detection caught every disposable address in our test set, which is exactly what you want if you are verifying signups at registration to keep bots and abusers off your platform.

The QUICK/POWER API

The API is the quiet workhorse. QUICK mode validates an email in about 0.5 seconds — ideal for a registration form — while POWER mode goes deeper for list cleaning. Users have integrated it with Make and other automation platforms, and the founder notes email verification can continue after lifetime credits are exhausted by switching to the daily-credit subscription.
