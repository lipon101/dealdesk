Amical review: the fast answer

⚡ Information gain — 30-second summary

Amical is a rare open-source dictation app whose local Whisper transcription is truly unlimited and private on every AppSumo tier: one hotkey drops formatted text into any app on Mac, Windows, iOS, or Android, in 100+ languages — and the $49 lifetime license covers your first 200,000 cloud words a month.

That is the short version. The longer version matters, because “dictation that adapts to the app you are in” is a bigger technical promise than it sounds.

Our analysis shows Amical earns its keep in three jobs: hands-free drafting, multilingual dictation, and privacy-first offline transcription. It is not a meeting-transcription tool yet, and power users will still want a specialist for long-form cleanup.

What Amical actually does

Amical is an open-source, local-first AI dictation app built by a two-co-founder Voice AI company in Vancouver. The company was founded on July 1, 2025, is bootstrapped with 1–10 people, and says it builds and runs its own AI models rather than wrapping someone else’s API.

Instead of a one-note speech-to-text utility, it layers four capabilities into one hotkey-driven app:

  • Dictation into any app — speak into any text field on Mac, Windows, iPhone, or Android, right where your cursor is, with no copy-paste
  • Context-aware formatting — the active app shapes the output: professional for Gmail, casual for chat, structured for notes
  • Local or cloud AI — on-device Whisper models for private offline transcription, or cloud models when speed and accuracy matter more
  • Smart shortcuts & vocabulary — custom hotkeys, voice commands, and a personal dictionary of jargon and proper nouns

Setup is a single download from the vendor’s site. The first-launch experience is genuinely simple: pick a language, choose local or cloud, and the app waits for a hotkey.

Feature deep dive: what stands out

The AppSumo listing promotes four headline capabilities. Here is what each one actually does in practice.

Context-aware dictation that formats for the app

This is Amical’s signature trick. The app detects the active application and formats your speech to match: a polished, professional email in Gmail, a casual message in WhatsApp or Discord, clean headings and bullets in notes.

The same sentence dictated twice in different apps can come out with different tone and structure. That is the feature working, not a bug — and it is the main reason Amical feels faster than plain speech-to-text.

Local-first, privacy-first transcription

Amical runs transcription entirely on your device with local Whisper models when you want maximum privacy, and can switch to cloud models when speed matters more. The company states it never retains your audio and never trains on your transcriptions, regardless of model choice.

The app is also fully open source, which is a meaningful trust signal for a tool that hears everything you type. You can inspect the code, and you keep access even if the company changes direction.

100+ languages, including mixed-language sessions

Transcription and dictation run in over 100 languages through Whisper, with automatic language detection or a manually set default. Mixed-language dictation in a single session is supported — rare among desktop dictation apps.

One caveat we verified in the reviews: full auto-detect can misfire when you regularly dictate in more than one language. The founders confirmed a multi-language selection feature is in progress for cloud models, and recommend setting a single expected language today for consistency.

Hotkeys, voice commands, and custom vocabulary

Productivity lives in the shortcuts. Amical supports configurable hotkeys for “Push to Talk,” “Hands-Free,” and “Paste Last Transcript,” plus custom shortcuts and voice commands that trigger frequent actions without touching the keyboard.

A custom vocabulary lets you add your own terminology, jargon, and proper nouns so industry-specific language is transcribed correctly, and the app adapts to your personal writing style over time. Integrations cover API, Discord, Gmail, Jira, and Slack today.

Frequently asked questions

Is Amical really free and open source?

Yes. The app is open source on GitHub, and basic local dictation is free. The AppSumo deal adds lifetime Premium access — cloud word allowances, team seats, and all future Premium updates — for a one-time price.

Do local models really have no word limit?

Yes. The tier table shows unlimited words per month for local models on every tier. The monthly caps apply only to cloud-model transcription, which is where the word pools come in.

Can I use Amical on my phone too?

Yes. The license covers Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android, and devices are unlimited across tiers — the limits are on team members and cloud words, not on how many devices you install.

What does pooling mean on team tiers?

All members on a plan share one monthly cloud-word bucket. On Tier 3, up to 7 people share the allowance — heavy users and light users draw from the same pool. Tier 1 has no pooling because it is a single user.

Does Amical work without an internet connection?

Yes. You can download local Whisper models in the app’s settings and transcribe fully offline. Cloud models are optional and used when you want faster or more accurate transcription, especially on mobile.

What happens if I do not activate my AppSumo license in time?

The deal terms require activation within 60 days of purchase. If you miss that window you lose the license, so redeem it immediately even if you plan to install the app later.

Can I get a refund if it is not for me?

Yes. AppSumo’s “We Got Your Back” guarantee covers this deal for 60 days, so you can test Amical against your own workflow and still walk away if it disappoints.
