What TruConversion actually does

TruConversion is an all-in-one analytics and feedback application that provides insight into website and mobile visitor behavior, helping businesses understand and convert visitors into customers. It is aimed squarely at marketers, growth teams and agencies who own a conversion metric and need to know why it is stuck.

Its toolkit splits into five areas:

  • Heatmaps — move, click, scroll and detail maps you can share, download, compare, and filter by date range, source, campaign, country, OS and browser
  • Session recordings — watch real visitors navigate your site to spot where they hesitate or abandon
  • Funnel analytics — visual builders with advanced targeting, no-code goals and a primary conversion-rate selector
  • Form field reports — see exactly which fields cause abandonment, plus which forms are not detected (see the caveats below)
  • Surveys & micro-surveys — ask visitors questions on-page and gather real-time feedback, plus a newer SitePulse AI reporting layer

The tracking code is a single JavaScript snippet that works across WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Magento, ClickFunnels and HubSpot, per the vendor’s install guides.

How we tested the platform

Conversion tools are only as honest as the traffic you feed them, so we tested on a busy test site and on a quieter staging domain to see how both behaved.

Our test protocol looked like this:

Baseline: install the tracking snippet on two test pages and verify with the dashboard’s “verify installation” check

Heatmaps & recordings: drive scripted visits and check move/click/scroll maps plus a replay

Funnel & forms: build a no-code funnel step, watch a form for field-level drop-off

Survey & AI: fire a micro-survey and review a SitePulse AI report against the raw data

The tl;dr of our testing: the data pipeline is solid, the AI report layer is a genuinely useful time-saver, and the form-analytics edge cases are the main thing to know before you buy (details below).

Feature deep dive

Three features deserve more than a bullet point, because they are where TruConversion either justifies or fails its price.

Smart Funnels: conversion experiments without a dev

Smart Funnels is TruConversion’s no-code experimentation layer. You build a funnel visually, target specific pages with advanced filters, and change copy, layout or offers per step — then watch individual page statistics to see which variation converts.

In testing, the standout pieces were the one-click share button for stakeholders and the conversion-rate recommendations, which nudge you toward the step that is leaking. It is not a full A/B testing platform — think “structured funnel audit” rather than “Optimizely replacement.”

Heatmaps and session recordings

The core maps — move, click, scroll and detail — all worked as expected, and the filters (date range, source of visitor, campaign, country, OS and browser) make them genuinely useful for diagnosing a single campaign rather than just a whole site.

Heatmaps can be shared with a link, downloaded, and compared against each other, which is exactly what an agency reporting to a client needs.

Surveys and SitePulse AI

Beyond behavior, TruConversion collects feedback with customer surveys and micro-surveys you drop onto a page. The newer SitePulse AI layer summarizes reports and gives you a prioritized read of what to fix first — we found its interpretation matched the raw numbers on our test funnel, and it is included in every AppSumo tier (at different monthly report counts).

Who should buy it (and who should skip)

👍 What stands out

  • All-in-one behavior suite — heatmaps, recordings, funnels, form reports and surveys in one dashboard
  • 1,500+ AppSumo reviews at a 4.91 average — rare, strong social proof for a marketing tool
  • Lifetime pricing that beats a $50+/month analytics stack within months
  • Agency-ready output — shareable and downloadable heatmaps, plus custom branding on higher tiers
  • Smart Funnels let non-developers test page variations with individual page statistics
  • SitePulse AI summarizes the noise into a prioritized action list

👎 Watch out

  • Form analytics has real limits — one form per page, no login-gated forms, and AJAX/iframe-loaded forms are not detected
  • Smart Funnels is not full A/B testing — think funnel audit, not an Optimizely replacement
  • Higher tiers get pricey fast — the $449+ tiers only pay off at real traffic scale
  • Data is only as good as your traffic — quiet sites will not produce useful heatmaps

Buy it if you run marketing for a site with meaningful traffic and you want one affordable dashboard for behavior, feedback and funnel fixes. Skip it if your forms are complex, AJAX-heavy, or behind logins — test the form-detection limits against your actual stack first (the 60-day refund makes that a safe experiment).

Frequently asked questions

What is TruConversion?

TruConversion is an all-in-one analytics and feedback application for website and mobile visitor behavior. It includes heatmaps, session recordings, funnel analytics, form field reports, micro-surveys, customer surveys, and Smart Funnels.

How do I install the TruConversion tracking code?

Copy the unique code from your dashboard and paste it just before the closing </body> tag on every page. You can then verify the install by entering your URL in the “Verify Installation” section, or by checking the network tab for TruConversion requests.

Can I use one tracking code on multiple domains?

Yes. The tracking code is universal and works across domains, subdomains and cross-domains, so you can track behavior across several sites with one snippet.

Can I use TruConversion with Shopify?

Yes, two ways: paste the tracking code into your theme.liquid file before the closing tag, or install the TruConversion Connect app from the Shopify App Store, which simplifies the integration.

What are the limitations of Form Analytics?

Form Analytics supports one form per page, cannot see forms behind login pages, requires forms to be inside a <form> tag, and does not detect AJAX-loaded forms or forms inside iframes.

What happens if I do not activate my AppSumo license in time?

The deal terms require activation within 60 days of purchase. If you miss that window you lose the license, so redeem it immediately even if you plan to install later.

Can I get a refund if it is not for me?

Yes. AppSumo’s “We Got Your Back” guarantee covers this deal for 60 days, so you can test it against your own pages and still walk away if it disappoints.
