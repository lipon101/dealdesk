What Switchy actually does

Switchy is a link engagement platform that has been on AppSumo since 2021 — the current campaign has 42,811 recorded purchases and more than 700 discussion comments, and reviews describe buyers using it for six straight years. Instead of just shortening a URL, Switchy attaches marketing actions to it: retargeting pixels fire when someone clicks, geo-redirects send visitors to the right country page, and deep links steer mobile users into the right app or store.

The feature set splits into five areas:

  • Custom retargeting links — unlimited links with editable titles, descriptions and share images, plus retargeting pixels for Google Ads, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Quora, Pinterest, AdRoll, Snapchat and more
  • Smart redirects — geo-redirection, link rotators, 404 redirection, A/B testing and custom slugs so one link can behave differently by visitor
  • Deep links — 150+ integrated platforms so a link opens the right page or app on any device
  • Smartpages — mobile-oriented mini-landing pages (a fuller biolink) with 100+ templates and 20 block types
  • Full tracking — analytics by country, referrer, device and browser, plus a UTM builder, tags, QR codes and GDPR-compliant popups

There is also a files manager, a notes field to monitor campaigns, and Messenger links for WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS and calls. The pitch is simple: the click itself becomes a marketing channel.

The results: link setup took minutes, the pixel drops fired correctly on click, and the smartpage builder is genuinely quick for a branded bio page. The catch is the click budget — every link, redirect and pixel event counts against your monthly clicks, so the tier math matters more than the discount headline.

Feature deep dive

Retargeting pixels on every link

The core idea is simple: when a visitor clicks your Switchy link, the platform fires the retargeting pixels you attached before redirecting them. That means a Facebook or Google Ads pixel sees the click even when the destination is another platform entirely. Switchy’s step-by-step setup walks you through the pixel IDs, and the same link can carry several pixels at once.

Deep links across 150+ platforms

Deep links let one URL open the correct page depending on the device and platform — an app page on iOS, a store listing on Android, or a specific website section on desktop. With 150+ integrations and smart routing, Switchy is closer to a full deep-linking tool than a typical shortener.

Smartpages and the biolink use case

The smartpage builder creates mobile-first mini-landing pages with over 100 templates and 20 block types. For creators stuck on a single bio link, a Switchy smartpage can hold links to ads, stories, QR codes, SMS and email in one branded page — and the lower tiers include 2 smartpages with the Switchy logo, rising to 30 branded pages at the top tier.

Geo-redirects, rotators and 404 handling

Marketers running international campaigns get geo-redirection (send each country its own landing page), link rotators to cycle destinations, and automatic 404 redirection so dead links keep working. Tags and notes help monitor which campaign each link belongs to.
