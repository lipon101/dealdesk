About this site

Why DealDesk exists

Software-deal marketplaces move fast, and most coverage is repackaged press releases. DealDesk tests the tools, names who they’re actually for, and is transparent about how the site earns money.

01How we review deals

Every DealDesk guide follows the same protocol. We install or sign up for the product with our own account, run it against a real workflow for at least a few days, and verify every claim we publish against the official AppSumo product page and the vendor’s site. Prices, tier limits and deal terms are checked on the day of publication.

  • We test before we recommend. No verdict without hands-on time — no repackaged press releases.
  • We name the limits. Every guide ends with who should skip the deal, not just who should buy it.
  • We flag uncertainty. If a feature is claimed but not yet long-term tested, we say “vendor says” rather than asserting it as fact.

02How the site earns money

DealDesk is funded by affiliate commissions. When you click a “Check the price” button or another outbound link marked AFF, we may earn a commission if you buy — at no extra cost to you. We do not accept payment for coverage, placement, or positive reviews, and an affiliate relationship never changes our verdict.

Full details are on our affiliate disclosure page.
