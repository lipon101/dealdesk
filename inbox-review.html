What INBOX actually does

INBOX is an email marketing platform created to give content creators a simple but powerful alternative to the category giants. It is based in Ontario, Canada, founded January 2014, with 11–50 employees.

Instead of a bare send button, it layers four capabilities into one dashboard:

  • Drag-and-drop campaigns — build emails with templates and a built-in newsletter editor
  • Automation — set up sequences and workflow automations that run on triggers
  • Segmentation & A/B testing — split your list and test what converts
  • Real-time analytics — open rates, click rates, and audience behavior as they happen

The company emphasizes compliance: GDPR, CASL, and CCPA are handled from a single account, which matters for Canadian and EU senders in particular.
