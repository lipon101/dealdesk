What Growify actually does

Growify lets you track attribution, creative analytics, customer journey, and budget allocation so you can scale your ads rather than your ad spend. Its AI-powered dashboard analyzes ad performance across all channels by campaign, creative and platform, and it is compatible with Shopify, WordPress, WooCommerce, ClickFunnels and any other website or CMS.

The platform splits into five areas:

  • Blended ROAS tracking — see return on ad spend across every channel together, not platform-by-platform
  • Pixel tracking — first-party pixel data that keeps working as privacy changes erode platform pixels
  • Customer journey — map the full path from first touch to purchase so no channel gets undeserved credit (or blame)
  • Creative analytics — compare ad performance by campaign, creative and platform
  • AI insights — recommendations on where to shift budget, generated from your own data

There is also budget allocation reporting, support for all global currencies, and unlimited workspaces and domains with multiple connected accounts. The pitch: most stores trust Facebook or Google’s own dashboards, but each platform claims every sale — Growify shows the blended truth.

Feature deep dive

Blended ROAS: the core promise

Each ad platform reports ROAS as if it deserves full credit for every sale. Growify reconciles conversions across channels so a store sees which mix of campaigns and creatives actually produces profitable revenue — the number that matters for scaling decisions.

First-party pixel that survives privacy changes

As cookies and platform tracking get restricted, first-party data becomes the reliable signal. Growify’s pixel tracking keeps attribution working and connects physical or cross-device journeys that platform pixels miss.

Customer journey and creative analytics

Journey mapping shows every touchpoint before a purchase, so you can stop over-crediting the last click. Creative analytics break performance down by campaign, creative and platform, telling you what to make more of — not just where to spend.

AI insights and budget allocation

The AI dashboard analyzes your metrics and recommends budget allocation across channels. Reviewers value it as a second opinion on weekly spend decisions — useful precisely because it is grounded in your own consolidated data.
