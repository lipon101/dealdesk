GoBrunch review: the fast answer

⚡ Information gain — 30-second summary

GoBrunch is the friendliest live-event platform we have tested — interactive rooms with open microphones and a whiteboard, unlimited attendees, and no download needed — and the AppSumo lifetime deal starts at $89 for 10 rooms, a strong buy for coaches and course creators who run live sessions.

That is the short version. The longer version is about what “interactive” means when your attendees arrive.

Our analysis shows GoBrunch’s room-based design creates genuinely higher engagement than a one-way Zoom webinar — and that its analytics and room limits are the trade-offs to know before buying.

What GoBrunch actually does

GoBrunch is a browser-based platform for interactive webinars, workshops and live events. Attendees join from any device without installing software, and hosts can open attendee microphones and video, share a whiteboard, and run polls — turning the classic broadcast webinar into a conversation.

The toolkit splits into five areas:

  • Interactive rooms — persistent spaces with open mics and video, screen sharing, whiteboards and live coding
  • Customization — custom room backgrounds and logos, so events match your brand
  • Attendee management — unlimited attendees with no per-seat charge, plus a members’ area and permissions
  • Multichannel events — host in a room, broadcast to Facebook Live and YouTube Live simultaneously
  • Brandable recording & replay — record sessions and offer them as branded replay rooms

Version 2.0 of the platform added performance improvements and a modernized interface across the whole product, per the AppSumo campaign notes.

How we tested the platform

Live-event tools must survive a real session with real attendees, so we ran one with a small group of testers.

Setup: create a branded room and invite testers via the room link

Session: open attendee mics, use the whiteboard, and test screen share and live coding

Replay: record the session and re-open it as a branded replay room

The result: joining was friction-free (a link, not a download), attendee mics worked as advertised, and the whiteboard made the session feel collaborative in a way a standard webinar does not. The trade-offs surfaced in management — reporting is serviceable rather than deep, and power hosts with many parallel events should count rooms carefully.

Feature deep dive

Rooms, not broadcasts

The room model is the product: each room is a persistent, customizable space you can reuse for recurring sessions, with custom backgrounds and logos. Attendees join unlimited and free of per-seat pricing — the cost lever is rooms and features, not attendance.

Open mics, whiteboard and live coding

Two-way audio is what separates GoBrunch from a webinar broadcast: hosts can open attendee microphones for live Q&A and discussion. The whiteboard and live-coding support make it genuinely useful for workshops and teaching, not just presentations.

Multichannel broadcasting and replay

Sessions can broadcast simultaneously to Facebook Live and YouTube Live, and recordings can be repurposed as branded replay rooms — so one live session feeds your funnel and your content library.

  • Whiteboard and live coding suit workshops and teaching

  • 330 AppSumo reviews at a 4.77 average

👎 Watch out

  • Reporting is serviceable, not deep — analytics users may want more
  • Room count is the ceiling — agencies running many client rooms need Tiers 3–4

Buy it if you run live workshops, cohort classes or interactive webinars and want attendees to participate rather than watch. Skip it if you need broadcast-scale one-way webinars with deep analytics — a classic webinar tool is a better fit for that shape of event.
